## Voice lines (voice/)
Generated with the macOS "Lekha" Hindi text-to-speech voice (male variants are pitch-shifted). Apple's licence allows this for personal, non-commercial projects.

## Sound clips (sfx/)
Scraped from myinstants.com. The meme dialogues are clips from Indian films and internet memes and are **copyrighted by their owners**. They're fine for a private, personal build, but must be replaced or licensed before this game is published anywhere.

## Generated in code
The town, auto rickshaw, weapons (lathi, belt, cricket bat), ₹ notes, title music and city ambience are all procedural or original.
